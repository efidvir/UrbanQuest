# Pictures for Udi's garden walk

Put the finished pictures in this folder and list them in the `art` block of the riddle file
(`riddle/riddles/garden.json`). Any picture that's missing falls back to the built-in drawing.

## Style (paste this first, with every picture)

Flat 2D TV-cartoon style: thick, clean dark-brown outlines; bright flat colours with at most one soft
shadow tone; simple rounded shapes; big expressive eyes; friendly, slightly goofy expressions.
No text, no letters, no logos.

Udi: a clumsy, kind theatre caretaker in his late 50s. Round face, big bushy brown moustache, grey
sideburns, teal flat cap, cream shirt with rolled-up sleeves, blue work overalls with brass buttons,
a big ring of keys on his belt, a yellow pencil behind his ear, a small brown notebook diary.

Mitzi: a chubby ginger tabby cat with darker orange stripes, green eyes and a mischievous look.

Make `udi-wave.png` first, then give it to the image tool as the reference for every other picture,
so Udi and Mitzi look the same everywhere.

## Cut-outs that stand on the ground in the camera view

Full body, centred, the whole figure in the frame with the feet touching the bottom edge, transparent
background (or a plain flat bright-green background if the tool can't do transparency), no ground shadow.
Square or portrait, at least 1024 px tall.

| File | Picture |
| --- | --- |
| `udi-wave.png` | Udi standing, facing the viewer, waving hello with one hand, the diary in the other, cheerful |
| `udi-read.png` | Udi standing, holding his open diary with both hands and reading it, puzzled frown |
| `mitzi-sit.png` | Mitzi sitting, facing the viewer, tail curled round her paws |
| `mitzi-walk.png` | Mitzi trotting, side view facing right, tail up |
| `key.png` | An old brass stage-door key on a small ring (just the object) |
| `cup.png` | An empty takeaway coffee cup with a lid (just the object) |
